What is FIRST Tech Challenge?

FTC isn't just about wiring motors or writing lines of Java—it is about learning how to fail, adapt, and build something awesome together. Every season, we start with a pile of raw metal and a game manual. Months later, we have a fully functional competition robot.


But we don't just stay in the workshop. We run our team like a real tech startup. We handle our own marketing, pitch to local sponsors, mentor younger kids in STEM, and learn how to solve impossible problems under serious pressure. It is the hardest fun we will ever have.
