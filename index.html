What is FIRST Tech Challenge?

FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) is a global robotics competition where our team is given a new game challenge every year. We work together to design, build, and program a robot from scratch to compete on the field. It takes a lot of dedication, problem-solving, and late nights in the workshop, but seeing our ideas come to life makes all the hard work worth it.


Beyond the robot, we operate our team a lot like a small business. We actively reach out to sponsors to fund our season, organize community outreach events to get younger students excited about STEM, and document our entire journey in an engineering portfolio. It is a challenging experience, but it teaches us real-world engineering, marketing, and leadership skills that go far beyond a normal classroom.
