What is FIRST Tech Challenge?

FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) is more than just building robots. It is a global robotics competition where teams of up to 15 students design, build, and program robots to compete in an alliance format against other teams.


We operate like a real-world engineering business. We raise funds, design our brand, do community outreach, and learn hands-on CAD, Java programming, and mechanical engineering.
